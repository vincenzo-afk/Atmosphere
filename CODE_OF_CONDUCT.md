# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Atmosphere project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include using welcoming and inclusive language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing empathy toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, publishing others’ private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The repository owner is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it. The owner may also take appropriate action in response to other behavior considered inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository owner’s [GitHub profile](https://github.com/vincenzo-afk). Reports should include enough context to understand what happened. The owner will handle reports respectfully and confidentially where possible.
